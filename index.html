Quick guide to using your tracker.

How to use 90 Days Track

Calendar: Tap a date to mark it worked. Tap again to unmark it. Use Previous and Next month to browse.

Notes: Add, edit, search and filter your study notes. Your notes are saved on this device.

Tasks: Tick tasks as you complete them. Your progress is reflected in the dashboard.

Study timer: Choose a duration and press Start. Keep this page open while the timer runs.

Backup: Data is stored locally in this browser. Clearing browser data may erase it, so use the export option below to keep a backup.
